okay, but really…

can my partner see what i tell it?

No. Never. Your conversations are two sealed rooms. The AI draws on a shared understanding of your relationship, but it will never reveal, quote, paraphrase, or hint at anything either of you has said.

if chats aren’t stored, how does it remember us?

After each conversation it keeps only a distilled understanding — the patterns, the context, what matters to you two. The actual words are gone. A great therapist’s intuition, minus the filing cabinet.

will it take sides?

No. It’s in your relationship’s corner, not either person’s. Because it hears both perspectives, it’s unusually good at helping each of you see the other’s — without ever revealing them.

is this a replacement for a human therapist?

No, and we’re careful about that. thrdwheel is a companion for everyday relationship care. For serious mental-health concerns or crisis situations, please seek a licensed professional or local crisis support.

what happens if we break up?

Either of you can dissolve the shared AI anytime, no questions asked. Its understanding of the relationship is permanently deleted for both of you. A clean goodbye.

how much does it cost?

One subscription covers both of you — it’s one relationship. Your first two weeks are free, and you can cancel anytime in two taps.
